Random Facts

  • Williams own the record of the most amount of points scored in 1 weekend. In 2014 in the season finisher in Abu Dhabi there was a double points rule in use that was only used for that race. During the race both drivers finished on the podium, Felipe Massa in 2nd and Valtteri Bottas in 3rd, meaning Williams tallied up 66 points in 1 weekend.
  • Between 1981 and 1982 Williams came up with a 6 wheeled car design, 2 at the fron and 4 at the back. This was because Technical Director Patrick Head calculated that the tall and wide rear tyres was causing roughly 40% of the cars total drag so he turned the 2 large rear wheels into 4 smaller rear wheels. This provided an insane drag reduction that gave the car a perfomance boost of roughly 160 to 180 extra horsepower. In addition all 4 rear wheels provided incredible traction out of slow corners meaning that the car could accelerate out of corners much faster than other cars. According to Alan Jones the car was exceptionally fast. Simulation data and subsequent tests from Keke Rosberg suggested the car could be up to 5 seconds a lap faster than the conventional machinery. However the car wasnt allowed to run in a grand prix due to the FIA growing deeply concered about escalating cornering speeds.
  • In 1996 after Damon Hill won his first title he celebrated the title with a promotional appearance where he drove his car through Disneyland and took a photo with Mickey Mouse infront of the Disneyland castle.
