In 1977, Frank Williams and automotive engineer Patrick Head came together and formed Williams Grand Prix Engineering. Having previously failed in the 1960s with Williams Racing Cars, Frank had little to no hope that Williams would be as succesful as it is today.

1978 was the first year Williams were competing in the championship, so were heavily expected to constantly be towards the back of the grid. Even with these setbacks,they had 2 drivers, Alan Jones and Patrick Neve. Alan managed to pick up 2 podiums and finish 11th in the championship.Williams ended up finishing 9th place in the constructors standings.

Then in 1979, with Clay Regazzoni driving alongside with Alan Jones, Williams ended up winning 5 grand prix and picking up 11 podiums. In the end Alan Jones finished 3rd in the standings with Clay behind him in 5th. This meant that in the constructors, Williams finished 2nd.

Next year in 1980, Clay Regazzoni left Williams and Carlos Reutemann replaced him. Combined, the pair had a total of 6 wins,14 podiums, leading to Williams first constructors championship, aswell as drivers championship as Alan Jones came 1st in the driver standing.

After winning the Drivers and Constructors title the previous year, Williams won the constructors championship for the second time. This time picking up 6 wins and 15 podiums, Alan finished 3rd and Carlos finished 2nd.

Then in 1982, Williams had a completely new driver lineup with Keke Rosberg and Derek Daly. Picking up only 1 win and 5 podiums, Keke still managed to win the drivers championship. However Williams ended up finishing 4th in the constructors.

1983,1984 and 1985 Williams where never the fastest or the slowest and only managed a few wins and podiums throughout those seasons. Through those 3 seasons they picked up 5 wins and the highest finish in the constructors was 3rd.

Then in 1986, With Nigel Mansell and Nelson Piquet behind the wheel, the team had a total of 9 wins and 19 podiums which quite comfortably secured them the constructors title. Nigel finished 2nd and Nelson finished 3rd.

Having a strong year in 1986, Williams had an even stronger year in 1987. Keeping the same drivers as the previous years, the pair dominated the field picking up 9 wins, 23 podiums and secured the constructors title once again. To add to these successes, Nelson won the drivers championship with Nigel close behind in 2nd place.

Having dominated 1986 and 1987, Williams started going downhill and didnt win another title until 1992 with Nigel Mansell and Riccardo Patrese. This was one of the most dominant years in the sport from Mansell, winning 9 races, finishing on the podium 12 times and qualifying first 14 times out of a possible 16 races. Patrese also had a good year picking up a win, 8 podiums and qualifying 1st once. Together they dominated the constructors championship and Mansell Dominated the drivers championship finishing a whole 52 points ahead of Patrese who was in second. Which is an insane margin when you consider you scored 10 points for a win. This means if there was an extra 5 races in the season and Patrese, who finished second, won them all, even if Mansell didnt compete in these races he still wouldve won the title by 2 points.

In 1993, Williams had a completely new driver pairing with Alain Prost and Damon Hill. With 10 wins and 19 podiums between them Williams had again won the constructors championship. Prost also won the drivers championship that year with Hill 30 points behind in 3rd.

1994 was meant to be a great year for Williams having signed Aryton Senna, often described as the best driver of his time. However Sennas career at Williams was nothing but trgaic. Having retired in the first 2 races due to car problems, Senna arrived at San Marino, the third race of the season , at the bottom of the championship. During practice, Rubens Barrichello had a big crash meaning he was no longer able to drive for the rest of the weekend. Then in qualifying Roland Ratzenberger had a huge crash and died that same day. Senna started the race in first after he came first in qualifying and was planning on showing tribute to Roland by bringing an Austrian flag into his car and he was planning on waving it at the race. Unfortunatley during the race Sennas car had failed to turn or steer meaning he went into the concrete barriers at top speed. After the race it was announced that the inertia from the crash had killed him.

Without Senna Williams signed David Coulthard to drive alongside with Damon Hill. Although it was a very tragic season, Hill finished 2nd and David finished 8th even though he missed the first few races. Williams did also in the end end up winning the constructors title even though it wasnt how they intended on doing so.

In 1995 Williams had yet another impressive year, with Damon Hill finishing 2nd and David Coulthard finishing 3rd. Even with those high drivers finishing position, Williams came 2nd in the constructors standing that year.

1996 saw Damon Hill winning his first title with Williams with Jacques Villeneuve in second. The pair had 12 wins and 21 podiums which secured them the constructors title.

In 1997 Williams had swapped Damon Hill with Heinz-Harald Frentzen. This was again another great year for Williams with the team picking up 8 wins, 15 podiums and another constructors title. Additionally Villeneuve won the drivers title with Frentzen behind in second.

Between 1998, 1999 and 2000 Williams didnt have a single race win and managed to pick up 9 podiums which was a surprise considering how good they were a couple of seasons prior. In 1999 the team signed Ralf Schumacher who Stayed at the team until the end of 2004, which was at the time the longest time a driver had stayed at williams.

Between 2001 and 2004 Williams slightly found their form again picking up 10 wins and 39 podiums. During those years they kept the same driver lineup of Ralf Schumacher with Juan Pablo Montoya.

Between the years 2005 and 2011 there was no race wins for the team and only 7 podiums. There were multiple drivers during these years including Nico Rosberg, Nico Hulkenberg, Rubens Barrichello, Mark Webber and Pastor Maldonado.

For the first time since 2004, at the Spanish grand prix, Pastor Maldonado somehow managed to win the grand prix giving Williams first win in 8 years. Even with that win Maldonado finished 15th in the standings and Williams came 8th in constructors.

In 2013, with Valterri Bottas and Pastor Maldonado, Williams managed to pick up a total of 5 points which was the joint bottom lowest point scoring season for the team tied with 2011.

However between the years 2014 and 2016 the team was looking better. Throughout those 3 years they kept the same driver pairing of Felipe Massa and Valterri Bottas. Between them they managed to pick up 14 podiums which was one of Williams best spells since the 1990s.

In 2017 Williams brought in Lance Stroll to replace Bottas as he went to Mercedes. In his debut year Stroll came 12th, scoring 40 points and picking up an impressive podium in Azerbaijan. Massa finished ahead of him in the standing by 3 points meaning he came 11th.

In 2018 Felipe Massa retired and Sergey Sirotkin replaced him. Together him and Stroll scored 7 points securing 10th in the constructor standing. Stroll finished 18th and Sirotkin finished 20th.

2019 saw a completely new driver lineup bringin in experienced driver Robert Kubica and young rookie George Russell. Kubica only managed to score 1 point which made up for all of the teams points in that season. Russell finished that season in 20th and Kubica finished in 19th. Williams finished 10th in the constructors for the second year running.

2020 wasnt any better for the team as Kubica was replaced by Nicholas Latifi. That season the team scored no points despite Russell starting 2nd for one of the races. Williams had for the third time running finished the season at rock bottom in 10th place.

Despite the recent form , in 2021 Williams technically managed to get George Russell onto the podium during the Belgian grand prix, however the race was stopped during the first few laps of the race making it unlikely that if it were a full race George would still finish on the podium.

2022 saw Williams bring in Alex Albon to replace George Russell. At this moment in time Alex is still at Williams and has resigned a contract to stay until 2028. Throughout 2022 to 2024, Williams managed to score a total of 51 points, with most of them coming from Albon.

In 2025 Ferrari driver Carlos Sainz moved to Williams as Lewis Hamilton replaced him at Ferrari. In 2025 Carlos picked up 2 race podiums and a sprint podium as well as an impressive front row start in Azerbaijan. However Carlos was still beat by Alex due to Alexs consistency throughout the season. In the end Alex was 8th with 73 points and Carlos was 9th with 64 points, securing Williams 5th in the constructors standings.
